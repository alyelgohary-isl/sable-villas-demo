What this website collects, why, and what the visualisations on it represent.

Who we are

This website is operated by Sable Villas, the developer of the twelve-villa lakefront collection at Jumeirah Islands, Dubai. Contact: sales@sablevillas.ae.

What the forms collect

When you register interest, request a visit or subscribe, we collect the details you type (name, email, phone, role, preferred layout and message) together with the page you sent it from. Submissions are relayed by Formspree to the sales team's email and used only to respond to you and to keep you informed about this development. Google reCAPTCHA v3 runs on the forms to filter spam; its privacy policy and terms apply.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing sales@sablevillas.ae.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the forms use Google reCAPTCHA, each subject to Google's policies.

Visualisations

All images and films on this website are artist's impressions produced for marketing purposes. They are indicative only: layouts, finishes, landscaping, views and surroundings may differ from the completed villas. Nothing on this site forms part of any contract. Specifications will be set out in the sale documentation.

Site terms

Content on this site is provided for general information about the development and may change without notice. Prices, availability, dates and specifications are subject to confirmation by the sales team.
