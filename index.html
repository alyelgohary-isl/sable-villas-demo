A small developer with one shoreline in mind.

Sable Villas builds a handful of homes at a time, on sites chosen for water and quiet rather than volume. At Jumeirah Islands that means twelve lakefront villas of four or five bedrooms, each with its own pool, handing over in Q4 2027.

We would rather you knew your neighbours than counted them. So the collection stays at twelve, every plot faces the lake, and each villa is designed around that view.
