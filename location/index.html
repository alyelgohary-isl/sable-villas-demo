Why Jumeirah Islands

A community that finished growing years ago.

Most of Dubai's new villas are in communities still being built around them. Jumeirah Islands is not one of those.

i

The lakes

The community is laid out as clusters of islands set in artificial lakes, so water and landscaping are part of the address, not an amenity added later.

ii

Established, not emerging

Mature trees, finished roads, neighbours who have lived here for years. What you see on the day you visit is what you get on the day you move in.

iii

Close to everything

Off Sheikh Zayed Road between Dubai Marina, JLT and the Springs, with the city's schools, clubs and beaches within a short drive.
